Import policy

Valid rows are inserted, active-email duplicates are skipped, and invalid rows are reported with their original Excel row numbers. Uploading or importing never authorizes automatic messaging.

Country names are accepted (for example Bangladesh, United States, and Australia). URLs may omit https://, and service_requested is optional, so older workbooks remain supported.
